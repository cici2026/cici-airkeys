## Full description (2711/4000)

```
Airkeys turns a spare phone into a cold wallet. The phone stays offline. The web companion at airkeys.app, in a browser on any computer, reads balances, prepares transfers and broadcasts them. The two exchange animated codes through their cameras, so no cable, network or Bluetooth connects them.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Turn on Airplane Mode and turn off Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Airkeys opens a wallet only while the phone is offline.
2. Type your recovery phrase or scan its SeedQR. Airkeys keeps it in memory and forgets it when you close the wallet or the app.
3. Show your accounts to the web companion as a code. It receives addresses only.
4. Prepare a transfer in the web companion and scan its code. Airkeys shows every recipient, amount and fee. Hold to sign, then show the signed transfer back to the web companion.

ADDRESSES ONLY
Most wallets give the online computer public keys. Airkeys gives it addresses, and puts every account on its own hardened path, so an account's public key stays unknown until it spends. Bunker mode moves what is left after a spend to a fresh account in the same signing. A quantum computer that breaks elliptic curves could derive a private key from a public key; with Airkeys, unspent funds stay behind keys nobody has seen.

NETWORKS
Bitcoin (native SegWit), and ETH and ERC-20 tokens on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, OP, Polygon, BNB Smart Chain, Avalanche and any EVM chain you add. Tokens Airkeys does not list are signed with a warning.

PROTECTIONS
- Works only offline: while a network connection, mobile data or Bluetooth is on, Airkeys shows only its offline screen. The app has no internet permission.
- Signs transfers only, after showing each one in full.
- Your recovery phrase is never saved. Screens that show it cannot be captured and are hidden in recent apps.
- The phrase field turns off keyboard learning and blocks copying.

BRING YOUR OWN PHRASE
Airkeys does not create wallets. It takes a standard BIP-39 phrase of 12 to 24 words, with an optional passphrase. It uses its own derivation paths, so other wallets show different addresses for the same phrase. The recovery guide shows how to reach your funds with Bitcoin Core and Foundry if Airkeys is ever gone.

OPEN SOURCE
The phone apps, their shared core and the web companion are open source under the MIT License. You can build Airkeys yourself for free; buying it gets you signed builds with updates and pays for development.

IMPORTANT
Airkeys is new (version 0.x) and has had no third-party audit. Start with small amounts and keep your recovery phrase backed up independently. Nobody, including us, can recover a lost phrase.

Requires Android 8.0 or later and a camera.
Website: https://airkeys.app
```
